## Story

**As a** maintainer,  
**I want** local secret artifacts cleaned and checked,  
**so that** secrets are less likely to leak through commits, packaged artifacts, or shared workspaces.

1. `.env.example` is confirmed sanitized.
2. Generated `.next/standalone/.env` is removed from local artifacts or documented as intentionally generated and excluded from packaging.
3. A secret hygiene check is documented or scripted for release/deploy preparation.
4. Git status/history check confirms no `.env` secrets are tracked.
